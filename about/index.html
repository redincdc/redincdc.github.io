About

Redouan Djeebet

Owner and creator of Red Inc

Redouan Djeebet is the owner and creator of Red Inc. He is based in Kansas City, Missouri, and works as an Associate at an Amazon Fulfillment Center in Missouri.

Background

He has a background in CNC and industrial sheet metal processing in Belgium, where he worked as a CNC programmer and operator and completed an internship at ASCO Industries, a Montana Aerospace company.

Education

He holds vocational certificates from Zavo Secundair Vrij Onderwijs in machine tools, computerized machine tools, basic mechanics in machine components, electricity and metal work, welding, and plumbing. He studied Applied Computer Science at Erasmus Brussel Hogeschool and Digital Arts & Game Development at Howest Kortrijk Hogeschool.

Interests and languages

He is interested in materials science, mechanical-electrical engineering, and industrial manufacturing. His native languages are English and Dutch. He also speaks French at an intermediate level and Arabic at a basic level.
